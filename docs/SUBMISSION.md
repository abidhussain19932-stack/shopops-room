Title: ShopOps Room: a shop's back office in one Telegram group
Track: Best Business Workflow
Description: ShopOps Room puts a shop owner and three Groq-powered agents (Sales, Inventory, Fulfilment) in one Telegram group through Switch. Sales posts paid orders and escalations, Inventory reserves stock and drafts supplier orders, Fulfilment ships and issues PayPal Sandbox refunds. Money-moving actions need the owner to type "approve"; agents can never approve. Switch handles routing, identity and permissions; agents are external WebSocket clients.
